## 0.2.0

* `rich` mood (`CritterMood.rich`): smug smirk behind shades, a gold ₹ coin bubble, a money bag and a coin stack.
* `CritterView(animate: true)`: breathing, blinking, each mood's body language and its floating effects (sparkles, notes, ₹ signs, rain, dust and moths). Off by default; respects reduce motion.
* Mood mouths are sized from the eyes as well as the art's own mouth, so they read on the cute frens.
* `moods_v6` golden; goldens re-baselined on Flutter 3.47.8.
